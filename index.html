Welcome to Pranada Ayucare

Rooted in tradition, close to home.

Pranada Ayucare is an authentic Ayurvedic clinic and wellness center in Aimury (near Perumbavoor & Kalady) providing effective Ayurvedic treatments, medicines, consultations, lifestyle modifications such as Yoga and meditations with our expert doctors.

How we can support you

Care shaped around you.

Ayurveda begins by understanding the person, not just the concern. Every treatment path starts with consultation and is adapted to your needs.

01

Ayurveda consultation

A considered assessment of your health, daily routine and individual constitution to shape a personalised care plan.

02

Panchakarma care

Traditional therapies selected and supervised according to your needs, readiness and treatment goals.

03

Spine & joint care

Supportive care for back, neck and joint concerns, with attention to mobility, strength and daily comfort.
